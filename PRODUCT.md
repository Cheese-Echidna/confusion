# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Mechanical designers familiar with Autodesk Fusion, working locally in a desktop CAD application. The existing repository implements planar sketching and parametric solids.

## Product Purpose

Confusion aims for familiar sketch, solid, component-organization and technical-drawing workflows. There are no cloud features. Designs preserve current parametric intent.

## Brand Personality

Minimal, precise, restrained. Fusion supplies workflow structure; Zed supplies the desired interface style.

## Anti-references

Cloud/account flows and unrelated manufacturing, simulation or electronics workbenches are outside scope. Avoid unnecessary permanent text, decorative panels and invented icon artwork.

## Design Principles

- Give the model most of the space.
- Put mode-specific tools in predictable positions.
- Use supplied icons, with names and availability in tooltips and menus.
- Make unfinished tools visible and honest about their status.
- Treat components as organizational collections of distinct bodies, not boolean unions.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

No additional user-specific accessibility requirements have been supplied. Buttons need discoverable labels; unavailable states must not rely on color alone.
